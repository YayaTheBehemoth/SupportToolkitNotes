# Endpoint Triage Ideas

**Status: proposed, not implemented.**

Potential workflows:
- Stale and long-offline endpoint monitoring.
- Correlated Intune compliance and Defender device/AV triage.
- OS version, Secure Boot, AV health and last check-in evidence.
- Incident-response assistance only after understanding and approving the underlying operational procedure.

Candidate categories: actionable, stale/offline, likely telemetry delay, insufficient evidence, escalation.

Open questions: stable device identity across systems; how long telemetry delays should be tolerated; evidence requirements; approval boundaries for remediation.
